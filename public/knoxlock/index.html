Questions

Is this right for my company?

This work is for founders who want to examine how their purpose holds up as their company grows. A fundraise, a new co-founder, changes to ownership or a board transition can all be useful moments to start.

Do I need to know the framework?

No. Start with the company you want to build and the decisions ahead. We can use the Incorruptible framework to structure the conversation without expecting you to arrive fluent in it.

Is this an official Incorruptible service?

knoxlock is Dave Knox’s independent consultancy. References to the Incorruptible framework acknowledge its influence. They do not imply affiliation with, certification by or endorsement from Eric Ries or the framework’s creators.

Does this replace legal advice?

No. The work clarifies strategic intent and governance priorities. Legal documents, tax decisions and regulated advice belong with appropriately qualified professionals.

What do you want to last?

Start with a short founder brief. Capture what matters, the pressure you anticipate and the decision you want to get right.
